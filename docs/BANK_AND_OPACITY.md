# 2.17.5: Spa bank alignment and Singapore solid road

Spa's positive transverse gradient was supplied as positive shader roll, but the shader's X rotation lowers local +Z when roll is positive. This inverted the bank: one side floated and the other penetrated the pavement. `roadSlopeAngles` now derives pitch and roll in the exact pitch-Z / roll-X order used by the car shader, including the coupling of pitch with bank. The existing reversed model axes for Ferrari remain handled by `carBodyFrame`.

On actual Spa asphalt, chassis slope now comes directly from the contacted triangle's analytic gradient. Sampling points beside a narrow road previously risked mixing the road plane with fallback terrain. Missing-asphalt fallback retains the smoothed height reconstruction and uses the corrected angle conversion. Existing ride-height and angle damping remain active; geometry and camera mounts are unchanged.

Singapore's source asphalt material 182 (`asphalt` texture) uses BLEND with texture alpha ranging from 21 to 153 out of 255. It was rendered in the transparent pass without depth writes. Ground materials 178/182/183/184/185/186 (paving, asphalt, grass ground, edge line and kerbs) now render as opaque with alpha=1 and depth writes enabled. Other material transparency remains authored. The shared imported-track shader also correctly ignores texture alpha for glTF OPAQUE materials, while MASK and BLEND retain their intended behavior.

Checks: `node tools/road_alignment.test.mjs` verifies that RB22/SF26 model-up and all four tyre contact points align with inclined planes across 90 pitch/bank/heading combinations. `node tools/contact.test.mjs` checks spatial triangle sampling and analytic gradients. `python3 tools/bank_opacity_smoke.py` checks thousands of real Spa road/model alignments and renders Singapore asphalt with a deliberately zero-alpha texture: the road must remain visible. It saves screenshots and results under test-results/bank-opacity. Camera tests and all 28 asset hashes remain required. Full-lap player driving is outside this targeted check.

Outcome: all checks passed. Real Spa geometry produced 2,058 model/plane alignment checks with maximum normal-dot error 3.33e-16. Singapore's zero-alpha asphalt still changed 7.5% of the tested viewport relative to removing that road entirely, confirming an opaque rendered surface. Main-road Spa and normal Singapore screenshots were inspected. No uncaught JavaScript errors were recorded. Five camera tests and 28 resource integrity checks passed.
